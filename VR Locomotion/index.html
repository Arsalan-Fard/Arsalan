Magic Broom VR Locomotion

Supervised by Daniel Medeiros

Created with Soheil Lotfi

A locomotion technique in VR that maps a rider’s leaning posture and controller input to the movement of a virtual broom—designed, implemented, and evaluated as part of the Human–Computer Interaction for Mixed Reality course at Télécom Paris.

Overview

The prototype is built in Unity. Leaning toward the tip of the broom accelerates, a controller button adds a smaller boost if needed, and rotating the controller at the tip of the broom steers up and down. A calibration step maps the physical broom and controllers onto the virtual vehicle.

The work was evaluated with seven participants through guided setup, exploration, a complete race, the Igroup Presence Questionnaire, the Simulator Sickness Questionnaire, and a short interview. Participants reported strong involvement and spatial presence, while the results also exposed moderate cybersickness.

An accompanying web experience documents the full course process.
